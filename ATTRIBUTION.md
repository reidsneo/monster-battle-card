# Attribution and prototype scope

This is a private, offline, noncommercial fan restoration of *Monster Farm Battle Card / Monster Rancher Battle Card*.

- The local `_MFBC_Rule-Book-v3.1.pdf` is the primary rules reference.
- Current card-face text is the authority for individual cards.
- LegendCup's published rules addendum is used to resolve interaction order and ambiguity.
- Original game names, artwork, translations, scans, trademarks, and related rights remain with their respective owners and contributors.

The source archive is not scraped at runtime, uploaded, or redistributed by the application. A public release requires separate permission from the relevant art, translation, database, and trademark rights holders.
